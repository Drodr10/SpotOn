The short version. SpotOn connects people who need to park with people who have a space to rent. To do that we hold your name and email, the vehicle you park, the spots you list, your bookings, and the messages you exchange with the other party.

We do not sell your information, we do not show you ads, and we do not use third-party analytics or tracking software. Card numbers never reach our servers — Stripe handles those. You can delete your account from inside the app.

4How we use it

We use what we collect to run the service, and for nothing else:

  • Creating your account and signing you in
  • Showing you parking spaces near where you are looking
  • Taking payment, calculating our fee, and paying hosts
  • Letting a renter and a host identify each other, the vehicle and the space
  • Sending booking notifications and account email
  • Investigating problems, disputes, fraud and abuse
  • Meeting our legal and tax obligations

We do not use your information to build advertising profiles, and we do not make automated decisions about you that have a legal or similarly significant effect.

6Payments and payouts

Payments run through Stripe.

When you pay for parking, your card details go from your device to Stripe. They do not pass through our servers and we never hold them. What we keep is the amount, the time, and Stripe's reference for the payment, so a booking can be matched to a payment for refunds and disputes.

When you are paid as a host, Stripe has to verify who you are before it can send you money. It collects that information — which can include your date of birth, address, a government identifier such as an SSN or tax ID, and your bank details — directly, on its own screens. We never receive it. What we hold is Stripe's identifier for your account and whether payouts are switched on.

Stripe is an independent controller of the information it collects this way, and handles it under its own privacy policy.

8Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information for as long as you have an account, and afterwards only as described here. We have not invented a retention period we could not honour: records tied to a payment are kept as long as we may need them for refunds, chargebacks, tax and legal obligations, and the rest goes when your account does.

Deleting your account in the app

Open Profile, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete my account.

Deleting your account after uninstalling

If you no longer have the app installed, email [ DELETION REQUEST EMAIL ] from the address on your SpotOn account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm the request and complete it within 30 days.

What deletion actually does

We want to be exact about this, because "delete" does not mean every trace disappears — and it should not, because some of these records belong to the person you dealt with as much as to you.

Your sign-in is disabled immediately and the account cannot be used again. What remains carries a placeholder instead of your name, and is kept because a booking and a conversation are the other party's record too, and because payment history has to survive for disputes and tax.

When we cannot delete straight away

We will decline the request, and tell you why, if you still have obligations open:

  • a booking of yours that has not finished
  • a renter with an upcoming booking at a space you host
  • money we have not yet paid out to you

Deleting in the middle of any of these would strand someone who paid for parking, or forfeit money you are owed. Once the booking ends or the payout lands, the deletion goes through.

10Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Access to the database is restricted per-user by row-level security, so one account's queries cannot reach another account's rows. Administrative keys are held only on the server and never shipped in the app. Passwords are stored as hashes by our authentication provider, not by us.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise. If we discover a breach affecting your information we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.
